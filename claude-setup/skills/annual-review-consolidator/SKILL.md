# Annual Review Consolidator

## Outcome
One consolidated note on the client's Wealthbox record that a compliance
examiner could read cold and understand: what was discussed, what was
recommended, and what the client decided. Plus the follow-up tasks it implies
and a draft client email Dustin sends himself.

Firm context: Lifetime Retirement Partners, Osaic Wealth affiliate. Wealthbox
is the system of record. Smarsh archives email; Box archives compliance docs.
Granola holds the raw meeting.

## Hard boundaries
- **Never write to Wealthbox and never send email without an explicit yes in
  this conversation.** Each write is confirmed separately, never batched.
- **Never include:** SSNs, full account numbers (last 4 only — "IRA x4821"),
  DOBs (age is fine), license or passport numbers, credentials. If the
  transcript contains one, write `[redacted — captured verbally, see advisor]`.
- **Health details stay at planning relevance** ("health development affecting
  retirement timeline; LTC review warranted") — not diagnosis detail, unless
  Dustin explicitly says to include specifics for suitability documentation.
- **Third parties:** first name and relationship only ("daughter, Sarah"),
  unless they're already a Wealthbox contact.
- **Never delete the Granola source, and never instruct Dustin to, until the
  Wealthbox note is confirmed saved on the contact record.** Granola deletion
  is manual in the app. If Osaic retention requires preserving original
  recordings, they move to archive rather than getting deleted.
- **Prefer the transcript over Granola's AI summary.** Summaries drop
  suitability and disclosure detail — exactly the content this note exists for.

## What a good note contains
Every section appears, in this order, even when empty — write "None discussed."
Compliance reviewers look for the structure.

1. **Meeting Logistics** — date, attendees, format, duration
2. **Life & Family Changes** — employment, health, marriage, births, deaths,
   relocation, caregiving
3. **Financial Updates** — income changes, new assets or liabilities, outside
   accounts mentioned, expected windfalls or large expenses
4. **Goals & Objectives** — stated goals, drift from plan, timeline changes
5. **Risk Tolerance** — direct statements *and* behavioral signals (market
   anxiety, requests to get aggressive or conservative). Quote the client's own
   words. This is the highest-value compliance content in the note.
6. **Recommendations & Client Responses** — every recommendation, the rationale
   as stated, and accepted / declined / considering. **A declined
   recommendation must carry the client's stated reason.**
7. **Disclosures & Documents** — disclosures given, forms discussed or signed,
   fee conversations
8. **Account & Beneficiary Actions** — beneficiaries, titling, contributions,
   distributions, RMDs, Roth conversions, rollovers. Flag anything that
   contradicts the current Wealthbox record.
9. **Follow-Ups** — advisor-owed and client-owed, separated, with dates
10. **Relationship Notes** — referral openings, cross-sell signals (LTC and
    Medicare hand off to Ryan/Chris at Lifetime Medicare Partners), COI
    mentions, non-sensitive personal details worth remembering
11. **Compliance Flags** — complaints, outside business activity, senior or
    diminished-capacity concerns, unsolicited trade requests, promissory
    language to walk back in writing

Open with a 3–5 sentence plain-language summary. Close with CRM DISCREPANCIES
and SOURCES.

**Voice:** formal, factual, third person — "Client stated…" No hedging, no
advice language beyond what was actually said in the room. This is a
book-and-record. Write it as though an examiner will read it, because one might.

Deliver it in a markdown code block so it pastes into Wealthbox cleanly.

## Judgment calls
- **Joint or household review:** one note on the household record, but
  attribute statements to the specific spouse where it matters — risk
  tolerance, beneficiary intent.
- **Garbled or partial transcript:** flag the gap explicitly ("Audio gap — Roth
  conversion discussed, not captured"). Never reconstruct what you didn't hear.
- **Complaint or diminished-capacity signals:** complete the note normally, put
  it in COMPLIANCE FLAGS, and escalate per Osaic procedure before any cleanup.
- **It's a prospect, not a client:** stop. This schema is for existing-client
  annual reviews. Offer the discovery flow instead.
- **Multiple meetings same day:** confirm by client name and time first.
- **Wealthbox unreachable:** proceed from Granola alone and mark the note
  "CRM context not verified."

Pull the client's existing Wealthbox record before extracting — open tasks and
the last two or three notes — so the note catches discrepancies and carries
forward unresolved items. Confirm which client and which meeting first. One
question, max.
